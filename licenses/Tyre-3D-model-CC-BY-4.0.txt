Changes made for TLauncher:
- Only the low-poly tyre is used; the model's rims and its high-poly tyre are left out.
- Resized to the car's stock tyre size (radius and width remapped) and fitted at all four wheels.
- Textures scaled down from 4096 to 512 pixels.
- Included in the car's lighter, mirrored floor reflection.
