https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2015-toyota-avanza-g-13-711adccbd846427b9dbca71e27fee50b

Changes made for TLauncher:
- Simplified to about half the triangles (meshoptimizer), with duplicate data merged.
- Scaled to 4.7 m long, placed with the wheels on the floor and the front facing +Z.
- The body paint material is named "paint" and has a clear coat, so the app can recolour it.
- Added a lighter, mirrored copy of the body (the floor reflection) and two studio floors
  (night and day) with generated textures. The floors are TLauncher's own work.

The model is provided as-is, without warranties. Use of the model doesn't imply endorsement
by the author, and this app isn't affiliated with Toyota.
